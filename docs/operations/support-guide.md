# Support Guide

For the support desk (phone, WhatsApp, in person at Dzaleka Online Services).

## Common requests

- **Can't sign in** — accounts use email one-time links / password. Resend the
  link; check the email address. No password reset flow yet — a fresh magic
  link signs them in.
- **List without a smartphone** — direct them to
  `/request-assisted-listing`, or take the request at the desk. A field
  representative visits, photographs and prepares the listing.
- **Where is my listing?** — check its status: submitted → verification →
  review → published. Providers see status in `/provider/listings`.
- **Report a bad listing / behaviour** — use the report button on the listing,
  or file it for them. Reports are confidential; never tell the reported party
  who reported.
- **Change my details** — `/account/profile`.
- **Delete my data** — log the request; deletion is honoured except where a
  record must be kept (see `docs/privacy/retention-policy.md`).

## Safety escalation

Anything involving immediate safety, harassment or protection goes to the
approved protection / legal-support pathway — not handled by the desk alone.

## Never

- Never ask for or accept identity documents as a condition of use.
- Never take payment on behalf of the platform — it holds no funds.
- Never share a provider's exact location or private contact without consent.
