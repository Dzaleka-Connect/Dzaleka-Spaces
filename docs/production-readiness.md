## Live-host observation

On 10 July 2026 the canonical origin returned HTTP 200 through Cloudflare and
Render with CSP, HSTS, frame denial, content-type protection, referrer policy,
and permissions policy. The observed deployment still returned
`X-Powered-By: Next.js`; `poweredByHeader: false` is present in the current
source and takes effect on the next deployment.

The observed response also referenced the previous bundled font assets. The
current release uses a system font stack so builds do not depend on Google
Fonts and the next deployment should no longer preload those assets.

## Release smoke test

1. Open `/`, `/spaces`, `/services`, `/verification`, `/privacy`, and
   `/sign-in` on mobile and desktop.
2. Confirm `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` use `https://spaces.dzaleka.com`.
3. Confirm account/provider/trades/verifier/admin pages are not indexable.
4. Submit a non-residential listing with a clean public photo and authority
   statement.
5. Assign a verifier, download the assignment, work offline, reconnect and
   sync. Confirm local evidence is deleted after successful sync.
6. Approve verification and publish. Confirm the public view contains zone and
   landmark only.
7. Send an enquiry with a permitted attachment, arrange a viewing, release
   directions after confirmation, and complete a safety check-in.
8. Record an occupancy, charge and external payment from each party. Confirm a
   receipt appears only after both parties confirm.
9. Create a maintenance request, quote, work order, completion record and
   review.
10. Queue an email, drain the worker, replay the signed webhook, and confirm no
    duplicate event or email.

## Stop-ship conditions

- Any protected flag enabled.
- Any exact household coordinate or private evidence visible anonymously.
- Staff operational access without AAL2.
- Receipt issued before dual confirmation.
- Queue worker or webhook running without its required secret/signature.
- Failed database/RLS/browser/build gate.
- Missing backup/restore evidence or no named incident owner.
